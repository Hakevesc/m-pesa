IF A TRANSACTION IS REVERSED
Points are reversed too

When a transaction that earned you points is reversed, the same points are removed from your balance and recorded in your history. Never more than the points originally awarded for that transaction.

GOOD TO KNOW

Points are never deducted until a redemption succeeds. If a redemption fails, your balance stays exactly as it was.

You cannot redeem more than you have. If your available points are not enough, the redemption is declined and nothing is deducted.

The same transaction only ever earns points once, and the same redemption is only ever processed once.

Every redemption gets a unique reference number so you can trace it in your history.

Only eligible merchants and agents can earn and redeem loyalty points.
